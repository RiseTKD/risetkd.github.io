Finchley Taster Session

Taster Intro

Ever wanted to try a martial art? Why not give Taekwon-Do a go!

Rise Taekwon-Do will be hosting a free, no-strings taster session at our Finchley class on Saturday 6th September! The session will be led by Daniel Beard (4th Degree), and we'll cover:

  • An introduction to Taekwon-Do
  • Fundamental techniques
  • Kicking drills and pad-work
  • Self defence releases/holds

Anybody aged 12 and up are welcome, regardless of physical ability or prior experience. The session will be completely beginner-friendly, and you can try it out for free and with no commitment!

If this sounds interesting to you, why not come by and give it a try?

How to Find Us

The Sports Hall at Christ's College Finchley can be tricky to find!

To get there, turn left through the car park at the roundabout as you approach the school building. Then follow the car park around the side of the building, pass through the basketball court and enter the building through the East Entrance. The sports hall will be on your left!
